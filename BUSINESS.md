# Brandform — Sales-Ready Business Brief

## Positioning

**Brandform makes small businesses easier to buy from.**

We turn unclear sales materials, websites and critical documents into tools that help a buyer, prospective client or internal stakeholder understand the offer and take the next step.

AI-assisted search is a useful diagnostic within one offer. It is not the headline product.

## Why this works

Small businesses often have a good product or service but weak commercial material:

- Retail buyers receive disconnected images, product facts and barcode information instead of a clear range story.
- Prospective clients encounter unclear websites, scattered proof and a difficult route to booking.
- Important PDFs attempt to be a catalogue, price list, proposal and order form at the same time.

The commercial problem is not “lack of design.” It is a lack of decision-ready information.

## The three offers

### 1. Buyer Kit Sprint
**For product brands selling through retailers, cafés or distributors.**

A focused sales pack that helps a buyer understand the range, commercial facts and next step.

Typical deliverables:
- Retail buyer one-pager
- Product/range sheet
- SKU, case size, EAN/barcode and wholesale information
- Sampling or first-order leave-behind
- Reusable template for future launches

**Founding pilot:** 6,900 SEK excluding VAT. One-pager, range sheet, one leave-behind, one revision round and final PDF files.

### 2. Client-Ready Presence
**For local experts and owner-led service businesses.**

An audit and implementation scope for the online information gaps that make suitable clients hesitate, choose somebody else or fail to book.

Focus areas:
- Website clarity and service explanation
- Credentials, proof and credibility
- Search, maps, profile and AI-assisted discovery diagnostics
- The journey from discovery to booking

### 3. Document Rescue Sprint
**For any business with a high-stakes PDF or document.**

A redesign of the content hierarchy, decision flow and production system—not decorative layout alone.

Typical targets:
- Price lists and product catalogues
- Proposals and quotes
- Onboarding and application packs
- Order forms and service brochures

## First market test

Start with Buyer Kit Sprint, because it is the easiest offer to explain, demonstrate and validate.

- Speak to five KAM / retail-sales contacts.
- Ask what information buyers repeatedly lack and where brands lose momentum.
- Show the fictional FJÄLLGLASS buyer-kit mockup.
- Aim for one warm introduction and one paid founding pilot.

The goal is a paid, useful client outcome—not design content or a high volume of leads.

## Guardrails

- Keep each engagement fixed-scope and decision-led.
- Never invent product facts, prices, claims, allergens, EANs, lead times or commercial terms.
- Use AI/search terminology as an internal method unless the client already understands and values it.
- Do not become a generic “we do websites, PDFs, branding and marketing” studio. Each offer must point to a specific sales or conversion moment.
